Official Guild Quest

Investigate the Lighthouse

Status: Open, accepting adventurers

Quest Description

The lighthouse at Cape Serrin has burned every night for four hundred years. Eleven days ago it went dark, and the keeper has not been seen since. Two fishing boats have run aground on the rocks below in the time since.

The guild is treating this as an investigation rather than a rescue, though adventurers should prepare for both. The coast road is passable but slow, and the weather this season has been poor.

Objectives

  • Reach the lighthouse and determine why the light has failed
  • Locate the keeper, or establish what became of them
  • Restore the light if it can safely be done

Additional Information

Parties of two or more are recommended. The guild will reimburse reasonable travel costs on presentation of receipts. Any items recovered from the lighthouse remain the property of the Keepers' Trust.

Quest Details

  • Reward: 12,000 gold
  • Location: Cape Serrin
  • Recommended rank: Gold
  • Posted: 10 August 2026
  • Quest type: Investigation
  • Expected duration: A week or more

The Guild Charter

Signed at Port Aldwin by the three founding companies, and binding on every member of the guild since.

  1. Every job goes through the guild, and the guild stands behind every job.
  2. Rates are set by the charter and published openly.
  3. No quest is posted without an assessed rank.
  4. Rank is earned by completed work, and cannot be bought.
  5. Any person may post a quest, whatever they are able to pay.
  6. Arrangements between members and clients are brokered by the guild.
  7. A member who abandons a quest without cause forfeits their rank.

The full charter is held at the guild hall and may be read on request.
