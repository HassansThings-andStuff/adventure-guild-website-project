The Guild Charter

Signed at Port Aldwin by the three founding companies, and binding on every member of the guild since.

  1. Every job goes through the guild, and the guild stands behind every job.
  2. Rates are set by the charter and published openly.
  3. No quest is posted without an assessed rank.
  4. Rank is earned by completed work, and cannot be bought.
  5. Any person may post a quest, whatever they are able to pay.
  6. Arrangements between members and clients are brokered by the guild.
  7. A member who abandons a quest without cause forfeits their rank.

The full charter is held at the guild hall and may be read on request.
