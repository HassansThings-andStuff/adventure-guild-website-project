About the Guild

Five thousand years of taking the jobs nobody else will.

Guild History

The Oceania Adventure Guild was chartered in Port Aldwin when three independent companies of sellswords agreed, after a long and expensive argument, that competing for the same work was making all of them poorer. The charter they signed set the rates, the ranks and the rule that has governed the guild ever since: every job goes through the guild, and the guild stands behind every job.

The guild has since expanded along the coast and inland to Greenhollow and Duskwater. It remains a members' organisation rather than a company, and its ranks are earned by completed work rather than bought. The full terms are set out in .

Organisational Aims

Fair work, fair pay

Rates are set by the charter and published openly, so that neither adventurers nor clients are left guessing what a job is worth.

Safety of members

Quests are assessed and given a recommended rank before they are posted, so that nobody takes on work beyond their training.

Service to the public

Anyone may post a quest, whether they can pay in gold, in goods, or in a week of bread from the bakery.

Training and advancement

Ranks are earned through completed work, and the guild supports members moving from bronze through to gold.

The Guild Charter

Signed at Port Aldwin by the three founding companies, and binding on every member of the guild since.

  1. Every job goes through the guild, and the guild stands behind every job.
  2. Rates are set by the charter and published openly.
  3. No quest is posted without an assessed rank.
  4. Rank is earned by completed work, and cannot be bought.
  5. Any person may post a quest, whatever they are able to pay.
  6. Arrangements between members and clients are brokered by the guild.
  7. A member who abandons a quest without cause forfeits their rank.

The full charter is held at the guild hall and may be read on request.
