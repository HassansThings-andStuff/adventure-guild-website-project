Yew Longbow

Weapons, bows. Made to order by guild artisans.

New

About this item

Cut from a single stave of seasoned yew and finished with horn nocks, this is the bow the guild issues to rangers on the northern roster. It draws heavier than a hunting bow and rewards the practice that takes.

Each bow is cut to the archer's draw length, so the guild asks for a measurement when the order is placed. A spare string and a wax block are supplied with every bow.

How it is made

Guild bowyers work to order rather than to stock, which is why nothing in the shop carries a stock count. A longbow takes roughly three weeks from measurement to collection, longer in the wet season when the timber is slower to season.

The Guild Charter

Signed at Port Aldwin by the three founding companies, and binding on every member of the guild since.

  1. Every job goes through the guild, and the guild stands behind every job.
  2. Rates are set by the charter and published openly.
  3. No quest is posted without an assessed rank.
  4. Rank is earned by completed work, and cannot be bought.
  5. Any person may post a quest, whatever they are able to pay.
  6. Arrangements between members and clients are brokered by the guild.
  7. A member who abandons a quest without cause forfeits their rank.

The full charter is held at the guild hall and may be read on request.
